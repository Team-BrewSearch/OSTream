# OSTream

OSTream is a cross-platform desktop frontend for [KHInsider](https://downloads.khinsider.com/), the largest repository of video game soundtracks on the internet. You can browse the entire catalog, stream music using the application, and download tracks to your disk, all through a single modern application that has a native feel.

## Features

### Browse & Discover

- Explore curated lists (Latest Additions, Top 40, Most Favorited, and Popular).
- Fast, full-text search across the entire catalog.
- Rich album pages complete with cover art, platform tags, and related albums.

### Audio Streaming

- Stream music directly in the app without opening a web browser.
- Supports high-quality audio formats including FLAC, MP3, OGG, and WAV.
- Full playback controls (play, pause, seek, shuffle, and repeat) with instant seeking.
- Integrates with your computer's system media controls (e.g., keyboard media buttons).

### Downloads

- Download full albums, individual tracks, or custom playlists.
- Choose your preferred audio format (automatically grabs the best available if your choice is missing).
- Automatically downloads cover art and neatly organizes your track filenames.
- Fast, parallel downloads that automatically skip already-completed files if your connection drops.
- Desktop notifications alert you when downloads finish or if an error occurs.

### Library & Playback

- Save your favorites and view your recent listening history.
- Manage your ongoing play queue (insert next or append).
- Fast, native app performance on Windows and Linux.
- Modern interface with animated transitions and full Light/Dark theme support.
